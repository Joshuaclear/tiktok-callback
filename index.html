Josh YouTube Uploader

Josh YouTube Uploader is a personal automation tool used by its owner to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channel, "Josh Butler: On Display."

The tool runs locally on the owner's own computer. It uses the YouTube API Services, with the owner's authorization, to upload video files from the owner's personal library and to read basic channel information needed to avoid duplicate uploads.

It is a single-user tool. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to or use it.
